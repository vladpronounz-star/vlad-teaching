What I teach

Build a skill by using it.

Lessons are adapted to the person in front of me. The goal is practical progress: speaking more naturally, understanding more, controlling the voice, performing with confidence, or turning an idea into something real.

OMT · Original Method

Learning by creating.

I developed OMT around a simple idea: learning changes when the student is actively creating. A lesson can move from conversation into improvisation, music, storytelling, visual prompts or performance—then return to the original skill with more awareness and control.
